Slide 4 — Where It Fails

Warning

Where It Fails

✕

Wrong Carrier Resin

PP carrier in a PE process = poor dispersion, gel formation, mechanical failure.

✕

Inconsistent Particle Size

Uncontrolled CaCO₃ distribution = die buildup, fish eyes, surface defects.

✕

Batch-to-Batch Variation

Different every time = constant process adjustment, scrap, and downtime.
