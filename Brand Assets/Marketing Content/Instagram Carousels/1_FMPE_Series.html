Slide 3 — LLDPE vs LDPE Carrier

Carrier Comparison

Why Carrier Matters

LLDPE

FMPE-1070 / 1075 / 1080
  • Higher tear resistance
  • Better elongation at break
  • Ideal for blown film
  • Bags, liners, stretch film
Best for LLDPE & HDPE blends

LDPE

FMPE-2070
  • Lower melt viscosity
  • Smoother dispersion
  • Better flow in LDPE systems
  • Coating & lamination
Best for LDPE-dominant blends
