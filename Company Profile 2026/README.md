# BLAU BATCH Company Profile 2026

A 17-slide 16:9 company profile, built from Brand Guidelines v5.2, the Visual Identity Guide v1.2 and the logo pack.

## Folder

| Path | What goes here |
|---|---|
| `Company Profile Prompts.md` | The Antigravity image prompt and the Canva deck prompt (Part A, slides 1–9; Part B, slides 10–17) |
| `images/` | Antigravity saves the generated images here (`cp01_…` to `cp07_…`) |
| `Slide Copy Sheet.md` | Slide-by-slide copy for building the profile from a Canva template |
| `exports/` | Save the finished deck here from Canva (PDF and PPTX) |

## Steps

1. **Images:** open Antigravity on the `Blau Batch Master` folder and paste §1 of the prompts file. It saves 11 images into `images/`.
2. **Canva setup:** follow §2a. You'll need:
   - the brand colours and Inter fonts in your Brand Kit
   - the logo-pack logos (`Brand Assets/Logos/BlauBatch_Logo_Pack/`)
   - the images from `images/`, the factory photo and the 8 website industry images, uploaded
3. **Build:** paste Part A, then use **Add slides** and paste Part B.
4. **Check:** go through the checklist in §2d.
5. **Export:** save the PDF and PPTX into `exports/`.

## Key facts used

- **Founded:** 2023. Egyptian company.
- **Locations:** office at Arkan Plaza, Sheikh Zayed City, Giza; factory at 79, 6th Industrial Zone, 6th of October.
- **Manufactured:** Filler masterbatch, 8 grades: FMPE-1070/1075/1080/2070 and FMPP-1070/1075/1080/2180.
- **White, Black, Colour, Additive:** through the Coraplast partnership. Standard grades, or produced to the customer's specification by Coraplast, ordered through Blau Batch.
- **Quality:** aligned with ISO 9001 principles (not certified).
