Every percent of Blau Batch filler masterbatch you dose replaces the same share of virgin resin. Work out your saving here in two minutes.

1

The principle

Saving per ton = Dosage % × (Resin price − Filler price) Filler masterbatch costs less per ton than virgin PE or PP, so the saving grows with every percent you dose.
