# BLAU BATCH Company Profile — Slide Copy Sheet

For building the profile from a **Canva template**. Each slide below gives the kind of template layout to look for, the exact copy to paste, the image to drop in, and the brand rules that matter on that slide.

Copy is final: facts come from Brand Guidelines v5.2. Don't let Canva AI rewrite it.

---

## Before you start (once)

1. **Apply the brand kit.** Open the template, go to **Design → Styles → Brand Kit → BLAU BATCH** and apply it to all pages. This swaps the template's colours and fonts for yours.
2. **Check every text style is Inter:**

   | Text | Inter weight | Case |
   |---|---|---|
   | Titles | ExtraBold | ALL CAPS |
   | Sub-headers | Bold | Title Case |
   | Card titles | SemiBold | — |
   | Body | Regular | — |
   | Small labels | Medium | ALL CAPS, slightly spaced |

3. **Delete the template's logo placeholder.** Put in your logo file instead (Uploads or Brand Kit):
   - **Dark slides:** `Horizontal_DarkBG`
   - **Light slides:** `Horizontal_LightBG`
   - **Footer:** small `Logomark_DarkBG` on dark slides, `Primary` on light slides
4. **Rules for every slide:**
   - **Amber `#D4840A`:** only on the small "MANUFACTURED" label (Amber fill, Deep Navy text). Never for text, lines or icons.
   - **Sky Blue `#2B8DD0`:** fine for text on white. Never for text on navy; use white there.
   - **Images:** remove any template photo you're not replacing. No stock photos, people or illustrations.
   - **Shapes and icons:** 4 px rounded corners on cards and buttons, thin Sky Blue lines as accents, navy outline icons.
   - **Brand name:** "BLAU BATCH" in titles, "Blau Batch" in sentences.
