Slide 4 — Higher Is Not Always Better

Warning

Higher ≠ Better

At 80% loading, you push the limits of dispersion. If particle size is not tightly controlled:
🔥

Die Buildup

Agglomerates at the die lip causing defects and downtime

👁️

Surface Defects

Fish eyes, gels, rough texture on finished product

💔

Brittleness

Reduced elongation, lower tear resistance, failure under stress

📊

Inconsistent Output

Thickness variation, weight fluctuation, rejected batches

Slide 5 — Carrier Resin Guide

Carrier Selection

Match the Carrier

LLDPE

Film & Bags
  • High tear resistance
  • Good elongation
  • Blown film, stretch
  • LLDPE/HDPE blends
Maintains flexibility at high loads

LDPE

LDPE Blends
  • Lower melt viscosity
  • Smoother dispersion
  • Coating, lamination
  • LDPE-dominant systems
Better flow in LDPE processes

PP

Injection & Thermoforming
  • Impact resistance
  • Toughness retained
  • Copolymer base
  • General PP extrusion
Balanced strength & processability

PPH

Woven Sacks & Strapping
  • Maximum stiffness
  • Higher crystallinity
  • Heat deflection
  • Raffia, tape, sacks
Rigidity where it is the specification
